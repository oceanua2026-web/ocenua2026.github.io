**Запитання:** Дай самостійну обґрунтовану відповідь щодо такого: what do you think the advantages and disadvantages of being the oldest or youngest child in a family are.

**Коротка відповідь:** Суть відповіді така: this is a discussion question; the article later gives possible advantages and disadvantages for eldest and youngest children.

**Повна відповідь:** It presents the youngest child as someone who likes attention and can learn from older siblings' experience, but who may become competitive and spoilt by too much attention. The article presents the eldest child as multi-talented, creative, confident, organised and often a leader, but also as a perfectionist who can put too much pressure on himself or herself and can be bossy.

**Приклад з підручника / Доведення:** For the eldest child, paragraph A says the person was born to lead and organise others, but may feel disappointed if he or she does not succeed. For the youngest child, paragraph C says older siblings can teach important things, but the youngest is more competitive and wants to show that he or she is as good as them.
